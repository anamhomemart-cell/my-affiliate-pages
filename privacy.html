Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. About this app

This app is a private tool used by its owner to schedule and publish content to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It has no other users.

2. What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives basic profile information (such as the account name and avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account. The app does not access the accounts of any other person.

3. How the data is used

This data is used only to show which account is connected and to publish the owner's own content. It is not shared, sold or used for advertising.

4. Storage

Access tokens and settings are stored on the owner's own computer or server. They are not given to third parties.

5. Visitors and viewers

The app does not collect personal data from people who view the published content or click affiliate links. Affiliate links lead to third-party websites, such as Shopee, which have their own privacy policies.

6. Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions.
